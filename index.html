Keyboard (when the player is focused)

  • Space / K Play / Pause
  • ← → Seek 5 seconds
  • J L Seek 10 seconds
  • ↑ ↓ Volume ±4%
  • M Mute
  • F Fullscreen
  • 0–9 Jump to 0%–90%
  • Home End Start / End
